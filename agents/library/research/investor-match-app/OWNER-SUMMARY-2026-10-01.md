# Owner summary: business pages + investor matching (2026-10-01)

**Next action (2 minutes):** reply with which of your five clients has asked about raising or borrowing money this year, how much, and for what.

## The plan
1. **Free public page for every business:** what they make, where they are, hours, photo posts. Customers read it on web and app. Useful with zero investors.
2. **Paid members' area:** businesses see which accredited investors fit their facts, with the reason shown, and talk only when both say yes. Investors join free.
3. **Flat price:** $29.99 or $79.99 a month, the same whether or not anyone connects.

## What the law changed
1. **No charging per introduction, per yes, or per deal.** That is an unlicensed finder fee. "Charge for matching" becomes "membership includes matching".
2. **No hand-picked introductions.** A fixed published rule picks fits; Elevare never pitches a business to an investor.
3. **No loan matching in v1.** It needs a broker registration in CT and VA. Main Street gets the page, a lender-ready packet and a free lender list.
4. **Nothing about a raise anywhere public.** A pre-post check blocks amounts, valuations and "invest in us".
5. **No scores or rankings.** Investors see dated facts: hours kept current, posts made, identity verified.

## Before any code
1. A securities lawyer answers 4 questions in writing.
2. An 8-week hand-run test in one metro: 20 businesses, 50 investors. Continue only if 12 businesses post weekly, 30% of requests get a mutual yes, 8 businesses pay, and 3 report a real funding step.
3. Then about 12 weeks of build, starting with a design board you approve.
